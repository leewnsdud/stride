# Race / time trial

Use explicit race intent, target and race strategy when supplied. A GPS/timer finish is not automatically an official elapsed result; stops and course measurement matter. Report target comparison only with compatible distance and timing basis. Without an official result, label it an activity-record comparison.

Review start restraint, comparable-distance splits, late-run change and execution of the stated strategy. The supplied early/late split estimate excludes incomplete km and may omit a middle km; it is not an official half-marathon split. Flat-road even pacing is a useful reference, not a rule overriding course gradient, weather, tactics or planned progression. Avoid declaring a fueling failure from slowdown alone; ask about intake, symptoms and conditions. For a trail race also apply trail guidance. One race can guide a follow-up benchmark; do not automatically reset training zones or forecast another distance precisely.

Evidence:
- [Pacing across marathon/half-marathon performance groups, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38187410/): observational split analysis of 208,760 finishers; pacing varies by event and performance. Associations do not prove an individual strategy caused a result.
- [Marathon pacing and training characteristics, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31671022/): observational relationships between training experience and marathon slowdown; avoid causal or universal prescription.
