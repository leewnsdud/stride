# Trail and trail races

Prioritize time on feet, ascent/descent, elevation density, stated terrain, effort and comparable sections. Whole-route min/km cannot fairly rank technical or mountainous runs against road running. Walking a steep climb can be intentional. Review climbing effort, sustainable run/hike transitions and descent control separately only when supporting segments exist. Elevation-density is ascent/distance, not average gradient. Do not convert it to race-finish prediction.

Computed terrain times use adjacent recorded samples, a ±3% grade classification, and reject large gaps/implausible grades. These are noisy descriptive estimates, not lab-tested thresholds or exact uphill duration. Report coverage. Unknown surface, altitude exposure, weather, poles, pack weight and aid-station stops remain unknown. Descent eccentric loading merits questions about soreness and control even with modest HR; HR alone does not measure muscle damage. Never infer muscle strength/body composition from running samples. For long efforts ask about actual fueling tolerance and hydration before prescriptive recommendations; no universal replacement quantity from calories lost.

Evidence:
- [Physiological Indicators of Trail Running Performance, systematic review, 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33508776/): heterogeneous courses/testing/populations limit cross-study comparisons and universal performance equations.
- [Performance Determinants in Trail-Running Races of Different Distances, 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35213820/): laboratory factors associated with race performance vary across distances. Observational associations do not validate predicting an individual's trail result from road pace or wearable values.

- [Downhill Running: What Are The Effects and How Can We Adapt?, narrative review, 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33037592/): downhill eccentric loading can have delayed neuromuscular effects. Exposure, gradient and training history matter; this does not diagnose damage in a specific recorded run.
