# Road / workout interpretation

- Easy/recovery: prioritize purpose, RPE/talk comfort, duration and configured HR target; faster pace is not automatically better. Low Training Effect can fit recovery.
- Long steady run: look at early/late comparable splits alongside terrain, stops, heat and fueling. Pace/HR changes suggest questions about fatigue resistance, not a diagnosis. A watch run cannot directly measure laboratory durability. Do not calculate decoupling from sparse excerpts; warm-up, pauses, changing speed and grade invalidate naive half-to-half ratios.
- Tempo/threshold/marathon-pace: compare the prescribed main-set duration/range and consistency, not average including warm-up. HR lags effort and drifts; an HR zone label alone does not verify a threshold stimulus.
- Intervals/repetitions/hills: evaluate identifiable work bouts and recovery separately, completed repetitions and late-bout change. Respect short-bout HR lag. Unlabelled kilometre laps cannot establish repetition completion.
- Cadence, ground contact, vertical movement: compare within the runner at similar speed/grade, not against universal targets. Changes do not establish economy or injury risk. Ask about sensor differences before cross-session power comparisons.

Evidence (reviewed 2026-09-15):
- [Maunder et al., 2021, durability perspective](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33886100/): proposes accounting for deterioration during prolonged exercise in physiological profiling. Conceptual guidance, not a validated watch-based cutoff.
- [Anderson et al., 2022, step-rate systematic review/meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36057913/): biomechanical changes occur with cadence changes; evidence for injury/performance effects and longer-term outcomes is insufficient. No blanket cadence correction.
- [Garmin Training Effect](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/garmin-technology/running-science/physiological-measurements/training-effect/): device-estimated exercise stimulus; distinguish stimulus from demonstrated future improvement.
- [Garmin Running Power](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/garmin-technology/running-science/running-dynamics/running-power/): algorithm uses running/terrain/environment inputs; preserve device context.
